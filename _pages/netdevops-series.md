## The Complete NetDevOps Blog Series
A 10-part guide taking you from traditional CLI networking to production-ready NetDevOps practices. Read the series in order or jump to the part you need.

### Series Parts
1. **Part 1 — What Is NetDevOps (And Why Network Engineers Must Adapt)** — Overview and urgency
2. **Part 2 — DevOps Concepts Every Network Engineer Should Know** — IaC, CI/CD, Git workflows
3. **Part 3 — Essential Tools for NetDevOps Beginners** — Git, Python, Ansible
4. **Part 4 — Python for Network Automation (Beginner-Friendly)** — Netmiko, NAPALM, examples
5. **Part 5 — Network Automation with Ansible** — Inventory, playbooks, roles
6. **Part 6 — NetDevOps CI/CD Pipelines Explained** — GitOps, Actions, testing
7. **Part 7 — Cloud Networking & NetDevOps** — Terraform and hybrid automation
8. **Part 8 — Monitoring, Telemetry & Observability** — Prometheus, Grafana, good alerts
9. **Part 9 — Building Your First NetDevOps Project (Portfolio)** — project ideas and structure
10. **Part 10 — NetDevOps Career Roadmap** — learning plan, certs, job roles
